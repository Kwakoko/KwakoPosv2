# Kwakoko Privacy, Compliance & Data Governance v1.0

Step 12 establishes the canonical governance contract for privacy, compliance, consent, data subject controls, tenant data isolation, retention, deletion, export, auditability, and AI-generated privacy claims.

The release gate is fail-closed. A release candidate must pass the machine-readable registry verifier, privacy governance unit tests, config build, and diff check.

This governance layer does not by itself claim regulatory certification. Legal and regulatory requirements remain jurisdiction- and processing-context-dependent and must be verified against applicable law before customer-facing certification claims are made.
