# Kwakoko Visual Identity v1.0

## Creative direction
Kwakoko uses a premium African technology aesthetic: intelligent, grounded, modern, confident and human. It must feel credible for a shop owner and serious enough for an enterprise boardroom.

## Canonical palette
- Kwakoko Forest: #0B5D4A — primary brand and action color.
- Kwakoko Deep: #071F1A — dark brand foundation.
- Koko Gold: #D4A72C — restrained emphasis and heritage accent.
- Koko Sand: #F3EBDD — warm neutral for editorial and marketing surfaces.
- Cloud: #F7FAF8 — light application background.
- Ink: #10231D — primary readable text.
- Slate: #5B6B63 — secondary text.
- Signal Blue: #2F80ED — informational state only; not the master brand color.
- Success: #138A63; Warning: #C88719; Danger: #C94A4A.

## Typography
Primary: Inter. Monospace: JetBrains Mono for technical identifiers only. Use strong hierarchy, compact labels, generous body line-height and avoid decorative typefaces.

## Shape language
Use calm geometric surfaces, medium corner radii, clear grouping, consistent spacing and restrained shadows. Avoid excessive glassmorphism, neon gradients, cartoon styling or ornamental African motifs.

## Koko visual language
Koko is the canonical African elephant ambassador. Artwork should be clean, premium and recognizable at small sizes. Use Koko as a contextual companion, not as a replacement for information or navigation.

## Logo behavior
The master wordmark is Kwakoko. The platform lockup is Kwakoko Business Operating System. KwakoPos may use a capability lockup only where the POS capability is the subject.

## Accessibility
Text and interactive components must preserve WCAG-friendly contrast. Brand colors must never be used as the only signal for state.

## AI generation rule
Any AI-generated Kwakoko visual must use these tokens and the canonical Koko specification. Do not imitate other brands, invent alternate mascots, or use stereotypical African imagery.
