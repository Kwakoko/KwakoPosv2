# Kwakoko Enterprise Customer Readiness Governance v1.0

## Purpose

Enterprise readiness is the controlled transition from a sellable platform to a successfully implemented, operated, supported, and expandable customer environment.

## Lifecycle

`QUALIFY → DISCOVER → BLUEPRINT → MIGRATE → TRAIN → PILOT → GO_LIVE_REVIEW → GO_LIVE → HYPERCARE → ADOPT → SUCCESS_REVIEW → EXPAND`

## Mandatory enterprise gates

- 40-pillar enterprise onboarding certification.
- Data readiness threshold of at least 70% before migration authorization.
- Source/target migration reconciliation before go-live.
- Ten-point enterprise integration certification.
- Attributable customer executive and Kwakoko lead approval.
- Ten-criteria go-live readiness review.
- Zero critical defects and zero tenant-isolation violations at authorization.
- Governed rollback and offline-queue preservation strategy.

## Support and operations

Enterprise support must preserve tenant boundaries, maintain persistent incident evidence, respect SLA escalation states, restrict autonomous remediation to approved actions, and require verification before resolution.

Hypercare exit is an evidence-based operational decision rather than an elapsed-time decision.

## Customer success

Success reviews use measured adoption, transaction volume, support burden, operational outcomes, and customer feedback. Certification fixtures and controlled simulations are not presented as proof of real customer outcomes.
