# Kwakoko Production Visual Asset Library v1.0

## Canonical identity

- Brand: **Kwakoko**
- Platform: **Kwakoko Business Operating System**
- POS capability: **KwakoPos**
- Ambassador: **Koko — African Elephant**
- Master tagline: **Run your business as one.**
- Koko line: **Koko never forgets your business.**

## Asset families

1. Corporate logo: primary, light, dark, monochrome and icon lockups.
2. Product marks: app icon, favicon and compact platform marks.
3. Koko character: welcome, guidance, insight, support, success, thinking, celebration and explaining poses.
4. Product illustration: onboarding, inventory, synchronization, reporting and assistant contexts.
5. Marketing: web hero, campaign banners, social cards and presentation artwork.
6. UI iconography: platform navigation and business-operation reference icons.
7. Brand pattern/display elements: restrained Koko/leaf/Africa-inspired supporting motifs.

## Production rules

Generated artwork is approved reference material for visual direction. Exact production logo geometry, semantic colors, typography, spacing, accessibility and product placement remain controlled by the canonical repository brand and design-system authorities.

Koko must remain professional, calm, intelligent, distinctly African and business-oriented. Koko must not influence authentication, authorization, security, destructive, financial-confirmation or other critical decision paths.

Do not distort, recolor, independently redraw or add effects to the corporate mark. Do not create uncontrolled Koko variants.

## Deliverables

The accompanying visual package contains master visual direction boards and reusable reference crops. The generated raster artwork is not the source of truth for exact wordmark geometry.
