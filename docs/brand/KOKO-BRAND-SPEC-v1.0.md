# Koko — Kwakoko Brand Ambassador Specification v1.0

**Status:** CANONICAL / LOCKED
**Identity:** Koko
**Species:** African Elephant
**Role:** Official Kwakoko Brand Ambassador & Business Companion
**Parent brand:** Kwakoko
**Platform:** Kwakoko Business Operating System
**POS capability:** KwakoPos

## Brand meaning

Koko represents intelligence, memory, strength, reliability, community, African authenticity, and business growth.

## Core promise

**Koko never forgets your business.**

## Personality

- Intelligent
- Calm
- Confident
- Trustworthy
- Observant
- Helpful
- Professional-first
- Warm without being childish
- Subtly humorous only where appropriate

## Product role

Koko is a contextual business companion. Approved product contexts include onboarding, education, synchronization status, inventory intelligence, business insights, empty states, feature discovery, release communication, and appropriate notifications.

Koko must never obscure critical business information, replace accessible text, interfere with transactional workflows, or appear as unnecessary decoration in serious financial or administrative interfaces.

## Visual governance

Koko must remain recognizable from silhouette alone. Future artwork must preserve the canonical character identity, proportions, facial language, posture language, and professional SaaS character direction.

Koko must not be redesigned as a childish cartoon, threatening character, stereotypical African caricature, imitation of another company's mascot, different species, or inconsistent character family.

## AI generation rule

Any AI agent generating Kwakoko artwork or UI containing Koko must treat this specification as authoritative and preserve the canonical identity. If an asset conflicts with this specification, it is non-compliant and must not be promoted to production.

## Machine-readable source of truth

The runtime definition is `packages/config/src/kokoAmbassador.ts` and its contract is `packages/contracts/src/kokoAmbassadorContracts.ts`.
